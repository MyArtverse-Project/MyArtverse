## Home & browse

![New landing page](/changelogs/062026/06262026-LandingPage.png)

- New guest temporary landing page with a short intro to MyArtverse.

- Signed-in users are sent straight to **Browse** instead of the home page.


## Comments

![New comment](/changelogs/062026/06262026-Comments.png)

- Redesigned comment threads with a cleaner composer and message bubbles.
- **Reply** to comments; replies appear nested under the parent.
- Sort comments **newest** or **oldest first** on artwork and profile panels.
- Timestamps use relative time (for example, “30 minutes ago”).
- Artist and staff badges shown next to commenter names.

## Artwork Header

![New comment](/changelogs/062026/06262026-ArtworkHeader.png)

- Artwork information now appears above the artwork.
- Icons has text added for clarity.
- **Copy URL** on artwork pages copies a shareable link to your clipboard.

## Favorites

![New Favorites](/changelogs/062026/06262026-Favorites.png)

![New Favorites](/changelogs/062026/06262026-FavoritesPage.png)

- **Favorite** characters from their profile and **favorite** artwork from the artwork view.
- Your profile **Favorites** tab lists characters you have saved.

## Follows

![Follows](/changelogs/062026/06262026-RelationModal.png)

- Follow and unfollow users from their profile.
- The **Relations** modal lists followers and following with quick links to profiles.

## Privacy & visibility

![Follows](/changelogs/062026/06262026-CharacterVisibility.png)

- Characters and artwork can be set to **Public**, **Unlisted**, **Mutual followers**, or **Private** when creating or editing.
- Visibility badges on studio grids and artwork cards show who can see each piece.
- Pages respect visibility - only viewers with access can open restricted content.

## Gallery & profiles

![New gallery layout](/changelogs/062026/06232026-Gallery.png)

- User profiles have a **Gallery** tab with folder sidebar, search, sort, and grid/list views.
- Gallery cards show character avatars, names, and Reference badges on artwork thumbnails.
- Character galleries use the updated card layout and filter toolbar.

## Artist credit

![Artist Credit](/changelogs/062026/06232026-ArtistCreditOne.png)
![Artist Credit on Artwork](/changelogs/062026/06232026-ArtistCreditTwo.png)

- Credit artists across platforms (Bluesky, X, MyArtverse, and more).
- Artist profile pictures are kept across platforms where available.
- Artwork pages show a **Visit** button linking to the credited artist.
- Artist search dropdown shows profile pictures and resolves Bluesky handles correctly.

## Characters

![Character Studio Cards](/changelogs/062026/06232026-StudioCharacters.png)

- Studio character cards use a cleaner grid design with filter bar and view toggles.
